The plan

One more reason to stay together

Every year, we make room for the conversations that deserve more time after the final session. This year will be no different. The BSides Kraków After Party brings our attendees, speakers, volunteers, partners and team together at SNY on Saturday evening.

The party is free, and drinks are on us. You do not need to buy a party ticket or pay for drinks covered by the vouchers available at the venue. You do need a wristband to enter.

Find us at SNY – Klub i Sala prób, piwnica SNY, Stefana Batorego 1, 33-332 Kraków. It is approximately 1.7 km from the conference venue, or 24 minutes on foot.

Entry

How the wristbands work

The venue has a maximum capacity of 180 people. Wristbands are limited, and there will not be enough for everyone at the conference. A conference ticket or badge alone does not grant entry to the party.

01

We will announce how to get an attendee wristband during the conference opening at 08:45 on 26 September.

02

Wristbands will be distributed during the event. A limited number will also be raffled during the closing session.

03

Staff, volunteers, sponsors and speakers receive their wristbands in advance. The remaining wristbands are available for attendees through the announced distribution and raffle.

Please take a wristband only if you intend to come. If your plans change, pass your unused wristband to another conference attendee so the place is not wasted. Keep it safe: lost wristbands cannot be replaced.
At SNY

Free drinks with vouchers

Entry is free, and the vouchers cover drinks at the After Party venue—there is nothing to pay for the drinks you get with them. Collect your voucher from party staff at SNY. Once you have used it, you may ask for another. Additional vouchers depend on availability; no fixed number per person is guaranteed.

A good night for everyone

Party rules

  • Bring your wristband. Entry is limited by the venue’s capacity and managed by the party staff.
  • Look after your wristband. There are no replacements if it is lost.
  • Follow the staff’s and venue’s instructions, and be considerate of other guests and neighbours.
  • The BSides Kraków Code of Conduct applies throughout the After Party.
Limited access180

Maximum people at the venue. Some wristbands are allocated to staff, volunteers, sponsors and speakers in advance.
