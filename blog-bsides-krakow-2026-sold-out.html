A historic first: BSides Kraków 2026 is sold out

22 September 2026 · 7 min read · By the BSides Kraków Team

For the first time in BSides Kraków history, every conference ticket has been claimed. With the full agenda now complete and four exclusive trainings opening the week, the community has turned the 2026 edition into a milestone before the doors even open.

The first sold-out BSides Kraków

This is more than a capacity update. It is a signal that Kraków’s security community wants a place where deep technical work, practical learning and open conversation belong together.

Every registration represents someone choosing to spend a Saturday learning, teaching and meeting peers. Some attendees are experienced security practitioners. Others are developers, students, founders, defenders or people attending their first BSides. That mix is exactly what a community event should create.

Thank you. To every attendee, speaker, trainer, volunteer, sponsor, partner and collaborator: this first sold-out edition belongs to all of you.

A complete agenda built for exploration

The programme for Saturday, 26 September, is now complete. The main stage runs across two deep-technical tracks—Discovery and Insights—covering offensive research, cloud security, AI, application security, detection engineering, identity, resilience and more.

Alongside the talks, four villages create smaller experiences within the conference:

  • Business Village connects technical reality with risk, governance and leadership.
  • AppSec & Red Team Village brings builders and breakers into the same feedback loop.
  • Cyber Games Village turns security learning into collaborative play and practice.
  • Women Go Tech Village creates space for careers, visibility, mentorship and connection.

Use the complete conference schedule or open the interactive agenda to build your day. Leave room for conversations: some of the most valuable BSides moments happen between sessions.

Exclusive training before the conference

The experience starts before Saturday. On 24 and 25 September, focused training sessions give participants time to work through practical security topics in greater depth. Training registration is separate from the main conference ticket, and availability depends on each course.

If you already have a ticket

We cannot wait to welcome you to the Faculty of Computer Science at AGH University, D-17, on Saturday, 26 September. Keep your QR code ready for registration and review the agenda before you travel.

Because the event is sold out, every place matters. If your plans change and you can no longer attend, please release or cancel your ticket so another person from the community has a chance to join.

The record belongs to the community

BSides Kraków was built to make cybersecurity knowledge accessible, practical and human. Selling out for the first time is worth celebrating, but the real success will be what happens next: ideas exchanged, skills practised, friendships started and lessons taken back to teams across Poland and beyond.

Tickets are gone. The agenda is ready. The community is coming. See you in Kraków.
