# Welcome — let's build together

Have a question, an experiment or an idea for the Hacker Club? Share it in **Project Ideas**. You do not need a finished plan or specialist knowledge.

Describe what you would like to build, what you hope to learn and how others could join. Upvote ideas using GitHub's native arrow, ask questions and volunteer in the discussion. Votes guide prioritization; they do not automatically approve a project.

Organizers review safety, feasibility, available resources and community benefit. Approved ideas get a linked Issue and a place on our [public Project Board](https://github.com/orgs/Security-BSides-Krakow/projects/1). The original discussion stays open for votes and conversation.

Bring curiosity. Share what you learn. Help the next person get started. Please follow our [Code of Conduct](https://bsideskrakow.pl/code-of-conduct.html).
