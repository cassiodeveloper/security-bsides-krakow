# Build Your Own Electronic Hacker Badge

### Description

Create a programmable electronic badge that combines a natural wood enclosure with accessible electronic components. Start with a small prototype, then share reproducible designs for members to customize.

### Goals

Learn circuit assembly, embedded programming and enclosure design. Produce an open bill of materials and a build guide.

### Potential Activities

Prototype LEDs and buttons on a microcontroller; compare wood finishes; run soldering and firmware sessions.

### Category

Hardware & Electronics

### Resources Needed

Microcontrollers, LEDs, batteries with suitable protection, soldering stations, wood fabrication tools and electronics mentors.

### Complexity

Medium

### How Can Others Participate?

Design artwork, test firmware, document assembly, source parts or join a beginner soldering session.

These are suggested activities for a community proposal, not an approved or active project. Scope and resources will be reviewed with organizers.
