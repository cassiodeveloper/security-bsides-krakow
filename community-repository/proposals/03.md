# 3D Printing & Prototyping Workshops

### Description

Help community members turn a sketch into a printable object through collaborative design, prototyping and practical workshops. Begin with simple useful objects such as badge cases and cable organizers.

### Goals

Teach basic CAD, slicing and safe printer operation. Build a shared library of reusable designs.

### Potential Activities

Run CAD introductions, compare prototype iterations and document material choices and print failures.

### Category

3D Printing & Makers

### Resources Needed

A maintained 3D printer, filament, CAD workstations, ventilation appropriate to materials and experienced facilitators.

### Complexity

Low

### How Can Others Participate?

Bring a design problem, help newcomers, test prints or photograph and document the process.

These are suggested activities for a community proposal, not an approved or active project. Scope and resources will be reviewed with organizers.
