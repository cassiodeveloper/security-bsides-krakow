The people who helped build the BSides Kraków Hacker Club.

Founder is permanent recognition. These first two Founders earned their status through their work building the community and making the Hacker Club possible. A Founder is a Founder, regardless of the path to that status.

Meet the Founders.

Founder #001 / 050

Cássio B. Pereira

Originator, founder & lead organizer

Cássio originated the idea and founded BSides Kraków. From the beginning, he has managed and organized the initiative across every area, taking responsibility for bringing the community, its activities and its operations together.

For the Hacker Club, his Founder status recognizes that sustained work and his role in turning the vision of a permanent community home into an initiative people can help build.

Founder #002 / 050

Fernando Cardoso

Co-founder & core coordinator

Fernando works alongside Cássio as his right-hand partner, coordinating, organizing and contributing across the initiative. As a co-founder of the idea, he helps turn plans into shared work and supports the community across its activities.

For the Hacker Club, his Founder status recognizes that hands-on contribution and his central role in helping make the Club possible.

Founder #001 belongs permanently to Cássio B. Pereira; Founder #002 belongs permanently to Fernando Cardoso. Founder status does not imply a particular current access level.

A permanent place in the story.

Founder status, number and historical recognition remain permanent. Once a number is assigned, it can never be resold, reassigned or reused.

Founders can request that their public name be hidden. Their number may remain visible where appropriate.

More profiles to come.

The wall will grow as additional Founders are confirmed. Fifty is the lifetime maximum, not a required target for the Club to open.
