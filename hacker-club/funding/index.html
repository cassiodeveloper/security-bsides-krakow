BSides Kraków Hacker Club is a community project run without a profit-making purpose. Every cent contributed by members goes towards building and sustaining the Club, our meetups and our annual BSides Kraków conference.

All membership funds support the community.Covering the costs of running the project is part of that commitment. Any surplus stays with the project to support its future.

The everyday essentials

What keeps the Club going.

A shared space needs more than a room and a set of keys. Membership contributions help cover the practical costs of making it useful, welcoming and sustainable.

A physical home

Rent, utilities, internet, cleaning, maintenance and the everyday costs of keeping our space available to the community.

Materials & equipment

Furniture, tools, cables, consumables, repairs and shared equipment for working, learning and building together.

Labs & experiments

Hardware, lab infrastructure and materials for hands-on security learning, research, workshops and community projects.

Food & refreshments

Food, snacks, coffee, tea and water for community activities. Sharing a meal helps people feel welcome, stay for a conversation and get to know each other.

Running the project

Payment-processing fees, administrative services and other operating costs needed to keep the initiative running.

Building for the future

Improving the space, replacing worn equipment and setting funds aside for upcoming costs and community activities. Money not spent immediately remains dedicated to the project.

These are the purposes membership funds support, not a statement that every category has already incurred expenses. Spending depends on actual needs and available funds.

Clear purpose. Shared responsibility.

A contribution to something we share.

Is the Hacker Club a profit-making project?

No. The project is run to build and sustain the community. Membership money funds the Club and BSides Kraków activities, rather than being distributed as profit. Any surplus is retained for the project.

Does every cent go directly to equipment?

No. Keeping the project running also means paying rent, bills, payment-processing fees and other operating costs. Food, meetups and the annual conference are part of the community we are building too.

Why does food belong in the budget?

Community happens around conversations as well as keyboards. Food and refreshments help make activities welcoming and give people time to connect. This is an explanation of how funds may be used, not a promise of meals with every visit.

Are there fixed percentages for each category?

No fixed allocation is presented here. This page explains the intended use of membership funds; it is not a financial report or a breakdown of actual spending. Needs and costs vary over time.

How can I ask about the use of funds?

Contact the team at contact@bsideskrakow.pl with your questions about a category or expense.
