01 / What is the Hacker Club?

More than an
annual conference.

BSides Kraków has always been about community, knowledge sharing and bringing security people together.

The Hacker Club extends that community beyond the annual conference and regular meetups with a planned permanent home. A place to work, experiment, break things, build things, teach, learn and meet other people who care about cybersecurity.

WORK

A community workspace for work, collaboration and meeting other cybersecurity professionals.

  • Coworking desks, Wi-Fi and monitors
  • Meeting and call areas
  • Coffee and shared workspace

HACK

Hands-on security labs, infrastructure and room to experiment.

  • Application, web and network security
  • Hardware, IoT, wireless and RF
  • CTFs, malware analysis and research
  • Lockpicking, relevant 3D printing and prototyping

LEARN

Continuous learning, led by the community.

  • Workshops and certification study groups
  • Lightning talks and research nights
  • CTF preparation and tool demonstrations
  • Member-led sessions

CONNECT

A physical meeting point for Kraków's cybersecurity community.

  • Community nights and networking
  • Speakers, researchers, founders and security professionals
  • Companies supporting the community
  • Career connections and collaborative projects

Planned facilities and labs will evolve with community participation, infrastructure and sponsorship.

04 / Community status

Your story. Your access.
Two different things.

Status recognizes your relationship with the community. Your access level determines how you use the Club today.

Founder

Permanent historical recognition, with a lifetime maximum of 50. Earned through a material role in building BSides Kraków or the Club, or obtained through the launch founding commitment.

Contributor

Recognition for active contribution. It can evolve with involvement and does not automatically make someone a Founder. Access may be granted separately.

Access

Supporter, Fan or Fanatic. Paid or granted through contribution or complimentary access. A Founder can also have no active access and still remain a Founder.

09 / FAQ

Hacker Club FAQ.

What is the BSides Kraków Hacker Club?

A planned permanent home for Kraków's cybersecurity community, extending BSides beyond the annual conference and regular meetups through labs, research, learning, coworking and collaboration.

Is this a traditional hackerspace?

It is a cybersecurity community club. Hardware hacking, prototyping and coworking support a broader focus on community, security research, experimentation and learning.

Do I need to work in cybersecurity?

No. Developers, students, researchers and cybersecurity enthusiasts are welcome alongside security professionals.

Can beginners join?

Yes. The Club welcomes people who want to learn and contribute. Lab access requires the relevant onboarding.

What is the difference between Status and Access?

Status represents recognition and community history. Access determines what you can use today: Supporter, Fan or Fanatic. A Founder or Contributor can have an access level granted separately, and a Founder can have no active access.

What is Founder status?

Permanent historical recognition, limited to a lifetime maximum of 50 people. It may be earned through a material role in building BSides Kraków or the Club, or obtained through the launch founding commitment. Both paths share the same public Founder status.

Can I lose my Founder status?

Founder status, number and historical recognition remain permanent when your access changes or ends.

Can Founder numbers be reassigned?

No. A Founder number can never be resold, reassigned or reused. If Founder #017 stops participating, that number remains associated with the same person.

What does the founding commitment cost?

5,000 PLN once, or 500 PLN × 10 monthly payments. Total commitment: 5,000 PLN for the same Founding Membership. It is not an ongoing monthly subscription. It includes permanent Founder status and number, exclusive recognition and 12 months of Fanatic Access. The start of the included access period is to be announced. Both payment options are available now.

What happens after the first 12 months of Founder access?

Founder status and number remain permanent. Further physical access and benefits depend on your current access level or a separate access grant. Choose Supporter, Fan, Fanatic or no active access.

Can Founders downgrade to Fan or Supporter?

Yes. Your access level can change while Founder status stays permanent. Billing and access-change procedures are TBD.

Can a Founder have no active access?

Yes. Founder #041 + No Active Access is a valid combination. Founder recognition alone does not grant ongoing physical access.

What is Contributor status?

Recognition for active community contribution, such as maintaining labs, teaching, mentoring, organizing CTFs or operating the Club. It may change with involvement and does not automatically mean Founder.

Can Contributors receive free Club access?

Yes. Fan or Fanatic Access may be granted separately based on contribution. Complimentary grants may also support organizers and important volunteers, without adding a paid tier.

Can I contribute instead of paying?

Yes. Meaningful participation can include time, skills and knowledge: run a workshop, maintain a lab, help with events, build infrastructure, organize a CTF or support community projects. Access grants depend on contribution.

How do Club Days work?

Fan includes 4 Club Days per month, with coworking during those days. Booking, day length and unused-day rules are TBD. Supporter does not include unrestricted coworking or lab access.

Can I bring guests?

Fan Access includes 1 guest pass per month. Fanatic Access includes multiple guest passes per month. Guest booking, limits and access conditions are TBD.

Who gets a guaranteed conference ticket?

Fanatic Access includes a guaranteed BSides Kraków Conference ticket if attendance is confirmed before the defined deadline. This includes the Fanatic Access period bundled with a founding commitment. Founder status alone does not confer the access benefit. The deadline is to be announced.

Do all 50 Founder positions need to be assigned before opening?

No. Fifty is the lifetime maximum, not a launch target. Unassigned positions may remain available during the early launch period; remaining numbers do not have to be sold when the programme closes. The confirmed allocation is 11 of 50 positions assigned, with 39 available as of 2 October 2026.

Can companies buy memberships?

Corporate membership bundles for security engineers, developers and researchers are planned. Packages and pricing are to be announced.

Can companies sponsor labs?

Yes. Lab sponsorship can support hardware, infrastructure, tools, equipment, furniture and development. The community remains independent.

When will the Club physically open?

The opening date and operating hours are to be announced.

Where will it be located?

Kraków, Poland. The exact location is to be announced.

Can I join now?

Founding Membership is available now for a total commitment of 5,000 PLN, paid once or in 10 monthly payments of 500 PLN. Supporter, Fan and Fanatic are not available for purchase yet; join the regular membership waitlist to hear when they open.
