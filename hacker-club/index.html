01 / What is the Hacker Club?

More than an
annual conference.

BSides Kraków has always been about community, knowledge sharing and bringing security people together.

The Hacker Club gives that community a permanent home. A place to work, experiment, break things, build things, teach, learn and meet other people who care about cybersecurity.

LEARN

Workshops, research nights, study groups and hands-on sessions.

HACK

Security labs, hardware, networks, CTFs and experimentation.

BUILD

Coworking, community projects and a place to turn ideas into tools.

CONNECT

Meet hackers, researchers, students, companies and security professionals.

03 / Contribute instead

BSides Contributor

Earned. Not bought.

People who actively help build the community may earn access to the Hacker Club through contribution.

Can't afford a membership? Contribute.

BSides Kraków is built on collaboration and contribution. Your place in the community should be about what you bring, not just what you can pay.

Help make it happen.

  • Run a workshop
  • Maintain a lab
  • Help organize events
  • Build infrastructure
  • Mentor other members
  • Create community projects
  • Support CTFs or research activities

08 / FAQ

Hacker Club FAQ.

Is this a coworking space?

Not exactly. Coworking is part of the Club, but the main focus is cybersecurity community, labs, learning and collaboration.

Is this a makerspace or hackerspace?

The Club may include hardware hacking and maker activities, but its primary focus is cybersecurity.

Where will the Club be located?

Kraków. Exact location will be announced later.

When will the Hacker Club open?

Details will be announced soon.

Can students join?

Yes. The Club is intended for professionals, students, researchers and cybersecurity enthusiasts.

Can companies join?

Yes. Corporate memberships and sponsorship options will be available.

Will there be cybersecurity labs?

Yes. Different labs are planned, depending on infrastructure, partnerships and community interest.

Can I help build the Club?

Absolutely. Contributors will be an important part of the project.

Can I become a member now?

Memberships are not open yet.
