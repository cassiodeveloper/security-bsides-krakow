BSides Kraków Hacker Club / Membership guide

Membership,
Status & Access

Your status tells the story of your relationship with the community.
Your access level determines how you use the Club today.

Recognition, current access and contribution each have a place. They do not have to be the same thing.

StatusAccessHow they combineFounding commitmentWhat stays

01 / Status = who you are in the community

Recognition and history.

Founder

Permanent historical recognition. A lifetime maximum of 50, across both routes to Founder status. Your number and historical association are permanent.

Contributor

Recognition for active community contribution. It may change over time with involvement. Contributor does not automatically mean Founder.

Same status. Different paths.

A Founder is a Founder. Public recognition and badges do not distinguish whether the status was earned or financially supported.

How someone becomes a Founder

Through significant contribution: granted to selected people with a material role in building BSides Kraków or the Club: original organizers, long-term core volunteers, sustained contributors and people directly making the Club possible. It may be granted without payment. Public status remains simply Founder.

Through the launch founding commitment: obtained through the launch founding commitment by financially helping build the Club. Both paths share the same public Founder status and the same lifetime limit.

02 / Access = what you can use

Exactly three standard access levels.

These determine current use of the Club. They are not permanent statuses. Regular memberships are not open yet. Join the waitlist and we will let you know when they become available.

Supporter

50 PLN / month

Open Club Days, community nights, member events and community benefits. No unrestricted coworking or lab access.

150 PLN / month

500 PLN / month

04 / What is permanent?

Recognition stays. Access can change.

Permanent

  • Founder status
  • Founder number
  • Founder historical recognition

Depends on current access

  • Physical Club access
  • Coworking and lab access
  • Fan and Fanatic benefits
  • Guest passes
  • Current membership benefits

05 / Why this model?

A community worth belonging to.

Some people bring financial support. Others bring time, knowledge and sustained contribution. The Club recognizes both, while keeping recognition and access clear.
