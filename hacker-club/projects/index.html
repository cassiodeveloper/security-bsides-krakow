02 / How it works

A spark. A team. Something real.

01

Propose

Share what you would like to build or learn. A simple description is enough to start.

02

Vote

Upvote ideas on GitHub and offer helpful feedback. Organizers review feasibility, safety and resources.

03

Build

Volunteer in the discussion or approved project Issue. Join a maintainer and learn together.

04

Share

Publish results, lessons learned and open-source resources for the next person.

Lifecycle: Proposed → Under Review → Planned → In Progress → Completed. Projects can move to On Hold when needed. Organizers may postpone or decline proposals based on feasibility, safety, resources and community benefit.
