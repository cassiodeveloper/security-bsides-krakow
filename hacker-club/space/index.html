Sharing the space

Build a place everyone can use.

The BSides Kraków Code of Conduct is our community reference. The following space guidelines are a draft; final operating rules will be confirmed before opening.

Respect people

Be welcoming, respect personal boundaries and follow the organisers’ guidance. Harassment and discrimination have no place in the community.

Hack with permission

Keep security experiments within explicitly authorised systems and lab environments. Do not test other members’ devices or the building’s networks without permission.

Care for equipment

Complete any required onboarding before using equipment. Ask before changing shared setups and report damage or unsafe conditions to the team.

Leave it ready

Clean your workspace, return shared tools and keep walkways and exits clear. Be considerate about noise during focused work and workshops.

Respect privacy

Ask before photographing or recording people, screens or ongoing projects. Do not share another member’s private information.

Coordinate your visit

Confirm guest and access arrangements with the team. Follow the on-site instructions for opening, closing and reporting problems.
