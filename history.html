2022

01 / The beginning

Every community
starts somewhere.

In 2022, the BSides Kraków story began: a local meeting point for people who wanted to share security knowledge, ask questions and learn from one another.

The first chapter brought the BSides community spirit to Kraków. The early archive describes an open platform for exchanging ideas and building relationships across the security community.

2025

04 / A wider conversation

From AI agents
to physical systems.

The 2025 programme crossed disciplines. AI security, EDR evasion, cloud defence, payment cards and automotive systems shared space in a single conference.

Discovery, Insights and Frontier formed three talk tracks. The AppSec & Red Team and Cyber Games Villages added another route into the day: practical work, challenges and play.

2026

05 / A milestone year

Sold out.
Still open in spirit.

For the first time in BSides Kraków history, every conference ticket was claimed. On 26 September, the community came together at AGH University for a full day of research, live demonstrations and hands-on learning.

Discovery and Insights ran in parallel with four Villages. Before the conference, four focused trainings on 24 and 25 September gave participants time to go deeper.

2026 / 01

The launch of BSides Kraków Meetups

A community.
Every month.

In 2026, BSides Kraków Meetups launched, bringing the community together for monthly meetups throughout the year.

The conversations no longer had to wait for the next annual conference. These regular gatherings created more opportunities to share knowledge, meet other security people and stay connected between editions.

2026 / 02

The launch of the Hacker Club

A home for
what comes next.

2026 also marked the launch of the BSides Kraków Hacker Club initiative: a plan to give the community a permanent home.

Built around work, hacking, learning and connection, the Club extends the spirit of the conference and the monthly meetups into a year-round project. Its planned space brings together coworking, hands-on labs, workshops and community events.
