# Code of Conduct

The [Security BSides Kraków Code of Conduct](https://bsideskrakow.pl/code-of-conduct.html) applies to our GitHub repositories, Discussions, Issues, pull requests and community activities.

Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, respect boundaries and privacy, and help others learn. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and unauthorized access to systems are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to **contact@bsideskrakow.pl**. Include the relevant links and context, but avoid unnecessary personal information. Do not publish allegations or personal details in an Issue. If your concern involves a maintainer, ask for a different organizer to handle it.

Organizers may moderate content, limit participation or remove access under the published Code of Conduct. They should handle reports with care, share information only as needed to respond, and consider conflicts of interest. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when action or disclosure is legally required.

For immediate danger at an event or in the Club, contact the on-site organizers and appropriate local emergency services.
