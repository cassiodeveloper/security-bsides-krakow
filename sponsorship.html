Sponsorship questions

Can we offer support other than money?

Yes. Nerd and Fan sponsorships can include a venue, catering, an after-party, or another contribution agreed with the team.

Can a sponsor give a talk?

Leading, Badge, Lanyard, Bag, and Nerd packages include a main-track or keynote slot. Contributing and Supporting include a main-track slot. These must be non-vendor talks, in English.

Do we need to send our logo or invoice details now?

No. Start with your contact details and preferred level. We'll collect marketing assets and operational details after sponsorship is confirmed.
